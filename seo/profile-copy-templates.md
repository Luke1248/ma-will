# Profile Copy Templates

Fill every `{{SLOT}}`. **Do not ship any sentence containing an unfilled slot,
and do not let me or anyone else guess at a credential for you** — an invented
admission date or award is a Rule 7.1 problem, not a typo.

Every template below is written to be compliant with MA Rules 7.1 and 7.4 as
long as the facts you insert are true. If you add superlatives, that stops being
true of the template.

---

## Keyword grounding (do this first)

Directory copy ranks on the language clients actually type, which is rarely the
language lawyers use. Before writing, list:

- **Service terms** — what you do, in client words. "Write a will", not
  "testamentary instrument drafting". "Someone died and left a mess", not
  "fiduciary litigation".
- **Geographic terms** — your city, plus every surrounding town you serve, plus
  the county, plus the courthouse names. `{{CITY}}`, `{{COUNTY}}` County,
  `{{COURT}}` Probate and Family Court.
- **Qualifier terms** — "affordable", "flat fee", "free consultation",
  "Spanish-speaking", "evening appointments" — whichever are true.

Your target phrases are combinations: *service + geography*, and
*service + qualifier*. Write those into the headline, the first sentence of the
bio, and the practice-area descriptions. Then stop — keyword stuffing reads as
spam to clients and to Google alike.

## 1. Professional headline (Avvo and Justia, ~60–100 characters)

This is the strongest single field. It is often the clickable title in search
results, so it must carry the primary service term and the geography.

**Template**
```
{{PRACTICE_AREA}} Attorney serving {{CITY}} and {{COUNTY}} County, MA
```

**Variants to A/B over time**
```
{{CITY}}, MA {{PRACTICE_AREA}} Lawyer — {{TRUE_DIFFERENTIATOR}}
{{PRACTICE_AREA}} and {{SECONDARY_AREA}} Attorney | {{CITY}}, Massachusetts
```

`{{TRUE_DIFFERENTIATOR}}` must be a fact: "Flat-Fee Estate Plans",
"Evening and Weekend Appointments", "Se Habla Español". Not "Aggressive
Advocate", which is both unsubstantiated and what everyone else wrote.

## 2. Biography

Structure: hook, credentials, approach, call to action. Front-load the keywords
into the first two sentences because that is what gets excerpted into search
snippets.

**Template**
```
{{FULL_NAME}} is a {{CITY}}, Massachusetts attorney representing clients in
{{PRIMARY_AREA}} and {{SECONDARY_AREA}} matters throughout {{COUNTY}} County
and the surrounding communities of {{TOWN_1}}, {{TOWN_2}}, and {{TOWN_3}}.

Admitted to the Massachusetts bar in {{YEAR_ADMITTED}}, {{LAST_NAME}} {{VERB —
e.g. "focuses their practice on" / "handles"}} {{SPECIFIC_MATTER_TYPES}}.
{{ONE_SENTENCE_ON_BACKGROUND: prior role, clinic, clerkship, or relevant
non-legal experience — only if true}}.

{{APPROACH_PARAGRAPH: how you work with clients. Fee structure if it is a
differentiator. Accessibility, language, scheduling. This is where a solo or
small practice beats a firm bio, because it can be specific and human.}}

{{LAST_NAME}} is a member of {{BAR_ASSOCIATIONS}} and {{PRO_BONO_OR_SERVICE}}.

To discuss a {{PRIMARY_AREA}} matter, contact {{FIRM_NAME}} at {{PHONE}} or
{{EMAIL}} to schedule a consultation.
```

**Worked example — illustrative only, verify every fact before use**

> *This example assumes an estate planning practice because of this repository's
> subject matter. If that is not your practice area, discard it entirely.*
>
> `{{FULL_NAME}}` is a `{{CITY}}`, Massachusetts attorney helping individuals
> and families with wills, health care proxies, and durable powers of attorney
> throughout `{{COUNTY}}` County.
>
> Admitted to the Massachusetts bar in `{{YEAR}}`, `{{LAST_NAME}}` focuses on
> straightforward, affordable estate planning for people who have been told they
> do not need a lawyer or cannot afford one — single adults, first-generation
> families, and clients with modest estates who still need their wishes recorded
> correctly under Massachusetts law.
>
> Estate planning does not have to be expensive or opaque. `{{LAST_NAME}}`
> offers flat-fee plans, explains each document in plain language, and returns
> calls the same business day.
>
> To discuss a will or estate plan, contact `{{FIRM_NAME}}` at `{{PHONE}}`.

Note what the example does *not* say: no "best", no "expert", no "specializing
in", no promised outcome. It is specific instead of superlative, which is both
compliant and more persuasive.

## 3. Practice area descriptions

Each directory lets you describe individual practice areas. Most attorneys leave
these blank. Filling them is free ranking surface.

**Template, one per area**
```
{{AREA_NAME}} — {{2-3 sentences describing the specific matters you handle in
this area, using client-facing language and naming the Massachusetts-specific
procedure, statute, or court involved. Close with the geography.}}
```

**Weighting.** Avvo asks for percentages summing to 100. Use 2–3 areas.
Suggested shape: `{{PRIMARY}} 60 / {{SECONDARY}} 30 / {{TERTIARY}} 10`. Eight
areas at 12.5% each ranks for nothing.

## 4. Avvo Legal Guide — outline template

Guides live on avvo.com's domain and can outrank your own site. Write the ones
your clients actually ask about.

```
Title: {{Client question, phrased exactly as a client would type it}}
       e.g. "What Happens If You Die Without a Will in Massachusetts?"

1. The short answer (2–3 sentences, plain language)
2. What Massachusetts law actually says ({{cite the statute — e.g. G.L. c. 190B}})
3. What this means in practice ({{concrete scenarios}})
4. Common mistakes
5. When you need a lawyer and when you do not
6. Next steps

Disclaimer: general information, not legal advice, no attorney-client
relationship formed.
```

Target 800–1,500 words. Two guides in month one, two more in month two.

## 5. Legal Answers — answer template

```
{{Direct answer to the question asked, first sentence.}}

{{The Massachusetts-specific rule or procedure, with a citation where one
exists.}}

{{The practical qualifier — what changes the answer, what facts matter.}}

{{When to consult counsel.}}
```

Rules that make answers rank and convert:
- Answer the question asked in the first line. Do not open with "It depends".
- Be Massachusetts-specific. Generic answers are what the other four responders
  already wrote.
- Do not pitch. The profile link does the selling.
- Never give advice that assumes facts not in the question.

## 6. Photo

Same professional headshot on Avvo, Justia, LinkedIn, Google Business Profile,
and your own site. Identical images across properties are an entity-matching
signal, and profiles without photos underperform badly on click-through.

## 7. Pre-publication compliance check

Before pasting anything live, confirm every line:

- [ ] No *specialist*, *expert*, *certified* — unless Rule 7.4 disclosure included
- [ ] No *best*, *top*, *leading*, *#1*, *guaranteed*
- [ ] Every award names its granting organization and year
- [ ] Any Super Lawyers / private-rating badge carries the Rule 7.4 private-
      organization disclosure
- [ ] Any past result carries a no-guarantee-of-future-results qualifier
- [ ] Every credential, date, and admission is factually accurate
- [ ] Name and office address present as required by Rule 7.2
- [ ] Testimonials reviewed against Rule 7.1
- [ ] Nothing true-but-incomplete in a way that misleads (the Rule 7.1 omission clause)
