# Practice Area Mapping

Your five stated areas do not map cleanly onto directory taxonomies. Three have
no category at all. Here is how to place them.

## Your areas → directory categories

| Your area | Justia category | Avvo category | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Appellate litigation | **Appeals & Appellate** (sub-services: Civil Appeals, Federal Appeals) | **Appeals** | Clean match on both. Select the federal sub-service. |
| Antitrust | **Antitrust Law** | **Antitrust & Trade Law** | Clean match on both. |
| Sovereign debt litigation | *No category* | *No category* | Closest available: Business / Commercial Litigation, or International Law. Describe it in free-text bio instead. |
| AI and professional ethics | **Professional Ethics** (exists in the Justia taxonomy) | Closest: **Legal Malpractice** or **Professional Ethics** if offered — verify at signup | The AI dimension lives in your bio and guides, not the category. |
| Social media class actions | *No category* | *No category* | Closest: Class Action, Consumer Law, or Privacy — **depends on plaintiff vs. defense side.** |

## Recommended selections

**Do not select five.** Avvo surfaces you only in the categories you pick, and
Avvo asks for percentages summing to 100. Five areas at ~20% each ranks nowhere.

**Recommended Avvo allocation:**

```
Appeals                    50
Professional Ethics /
  Legal Malpractice        30
Antitrust & Trade Law      20
```

**Recommended Justia selections:** Appeals & Appellate (Federal Appeals),
Professional Ethics, Antitrust Law.

Rationale: these are the three that (a) exist as categories, (b) have any search
demand, and (c) are the ones where a directory listing plausibly gets read.
Sovereign debt and social media class actions belong in your bio free text,
where they read as substance rather than as a diluted category claim.

If the class action work is **plaintiff-side**, revisit this — add Class Action
and weight it heavily, because that is the one part of your practice with real
claimant search volume.

## Free-text placement for the unmapped areas

The two areas without categories still belong on the profile — just in prose,
where they do useful work as differentiators and as long-tail keyword surface:

> `{{ROLE VERB — e.g. "Represents" / "Advises" / "Has served as part of teams
> representing"}}` institutional creditors and sovereign entities in
> cross-border debt restructuring and enforcement litigation, and
> `{{ROLE VERB}}` in class litigation arising from social media platform
> practices.

Pick the role verb that is accurate. See the Rule 7.1 note in
`channel-strategy.md` — the risk in this practice mix is describing matter types
without describing your role in them.

## Keyword targets that are actually worth having

Forget "Boston lawyer near me". For this practice, the realistic search targets
are topical and professional-audience:

**AI and professional ethics** (your best surface):
- "AI hallucination sanctions Massachusetts"
- "attorney ethics generative AI Massachusetts"
- "Rule 1.1 competence artificial intelligence lawyers"
- "can lawyers use ChatGPT Massachusetts"
- "law firm AI policy ethics opinion"

**Appellate**:
- "Massachusetts Appeals Court brief" / "SJC appellate counsel"
- "First Circuit appellate attorney Boston"
- "appellate counsel referral Massachusetts"

**Antitrust** — low volume, largely institutional. Do not build content here for
search; build it for Chambers submissions and Law360 bylines instead.

The AI ethics cluster is the only one where a written piece has a real chance of
ranking and being read by someone who might hire you. If you write four things
this year, write them there.
