# Legal Directory Profile SEO Kit

A reusable kit for building attorney directory profiles in Massachusetts, with
a channel-fit analysis for practices whose clients are institutionally sourced.

## First: check whether the profiles exist

Search your name on Avvo and Justia before anything else. Avvo auto-generates
listings from public bar records, but coverage is incomplete and recently
admitted attorneys are frequently missing. Justia will create a free profile on
request if none exists.

The work is different in each case — claiming an existing listing is a ten
minute task, creating one from scratch is an afternoon — so establish which you
are doing first.

**Worked example used throughout:** an institutional litigation practice —
appellate, antitrust, sovereign debt, AI and professional responsibility, and
class actions. Substitute your own areas; the reasoning transfers.

---

## Read this first

**`channel-strategy.md` is the most important file here, and it argues against
part of what you asked for.** In short: directory SEO is a poor fit for four of
your five practice areas. Nobody sources sovereign debt counsel from Avvo.
Antitrust and appellate work arrives through Chambers, Legal 500, GCR,
Benchmark, and referrals from trial counsel.

The recommendation is not "don't do it" — it is **do it once, defensively, then
put the recurring effort somewhere it pays.** Create both profiles to control
your name in search results, fill them completely, and stop. Then redirect the
90 days into scholarship, bar section work, CLE speaking, and Chambers
submissions, which also happen to be exactly what the Super Lawyers research
department scans.

Your **AI and professional ethics** work is the exception and the opportunity —
a thin-competition niche with live regulatory movement and an audience that
actually searches. That is where to invest.

## What this kit cannot do

I have no authenticated access to Avvo, Justia, or Super Lawyers — they are
third-party platforms behind logins. I research, audit, and draft; you paste.
Nothing here touches a live profile.

Remaining `{{PLACEHOLDER}}` slots are facts I do not have: firm name, office
address, phone, admission year, law school, bar memberships. Do not ship a
sentence containing an unfilled slot, and do not let anyone — including me —
guess at a credential on your behalf.

## Open question that changes the plan

**Are the social media class actions plaintiff-side or defense-side?** These are
opposite businesses with opposite marketing. Plaintiff-side means claimant
intake where search visibility genuinely converts; defense-side means corporate
counsel selects you and directories are irrelevant. Roughly half the
recommendations in `channel-strategy.md` and `practice-area-mapping.md` turn on
this.
